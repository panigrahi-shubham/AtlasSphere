About SkyAtlas

SkyAtlas is a non-commercial geography and weather QA portfolio project. No ads or payments.

Forecasts are model estimates from Open-Meteo, not weather warnings. Country details and photographs are reference snapshots. The news preview is fixture content, not live news.

Browser storage holds theme, units and favorites. Search and weather requests send coordinates or search text to Open-Meteo; GPS is requested only when you select Use my location.
